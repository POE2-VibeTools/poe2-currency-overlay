What to align

Lay your box directly on top of the stash panel's coloured outer bounding box (the glowing border). When your lines cover the coloured lines, it's aligned.

⚠️ For reliable reads, run the game at 1920×1080 or larger. Smaller panels shrink the count digits and the scan starts missing them.
Drag the box roughly over your stash panel, then hit Auto-snap - each edge locks onto the coloured bounding line. Fine-tune by hand if needed (the magnifier highlights the exact pixel row/column). Then Confirm.
