Targeting ~2.7

Dedicated Arbitrage Route tabPlanned

A tab where you can build and check your own trade routes, instead of only viewing the single best route the app finds for a pair.

In-app bug reportingPlanned

Report a specific thing that misbehaved - a currency row, a search, a result, a desecration - straight from the app, with the real underlying data attached.

Recently shipped

Five more languagesShipped 2.6.0

The app runs in Russian, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French or Spanish, and price checks items copied from those clients. Settings → App → Language.

Regex BuilderShipped 2.5.0

Build stash-search regex for waystones and tablets - real roll ranges, thresholds, exclusions, seed from a copied item - and save them under your own labels for 1-click copy.

Grand Expedition plannerShipped 2.5.0

Tap the rumors you see, get a SPEND/SAVE verdict on your Aldurs from community island ratings, copy a map-note tag that rebuilds the roster, and prep with in-game item windows + trade links.

Command HotkeysShipped 2.5.0

Bind a hotkey to a safe in-game chat command like /hideout - or your own /command with arguments. One key, one manually-triggered command - nothing GGG's rules disallow.

Tab managementShipped 2.5.0

Drag the tab bar into your order, hide the tabs you don't use with their ✕, and turn them back on in Settings.

Richer Search History rowsShipped 2.4.0

Price Check history rows show the item's icon and its suggested floor price, so the list reads at a glance.

Net Worth (Stash Auto-Calculator)Shipped 2.4.0

Open a currency tab, press F7, and the app reads the counts and totals what it's worth - a running grand total in Exalted and Divine with a Mirror count for the rich. Covers Currency, Abyss, Ritual, Essence, all 5 Rune subtabs, Delirium, Breach and Expedition. Best at 1920x1080+; calibrate once for any other size.

Reorder currencies within a bucketShipped 2.3.4

Drag the currencies inside a bucket to reorder them, so each bucket sits the way you like instead of the default order.

Multi-select when adding to a bucketShipped 2.3.4

Add several currencies to a bucket at once with multi-select, instead of one at a time.

Paginate history listsShipped 2.3.4

Proper pagination for Price Check and Desecrate history, instead of a "load more" button that grows one endlessly long list.

Dyslexia-friendly font toggleShipped 2.3.5

A setting to switch the app's font to a dyslexia-friendly typeface. Community-suggested, pairing with the currency-icon option.
